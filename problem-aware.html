BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The questions
your finance team
will ask.

Returns, access, risk and fees. Get clear on each one.

Can we get the money back for payroll or GST?

Liquid and overnight funds typically settle on the next business day, subject to cut-offs, holidays and scheme terms. Keep cash for immediate payments in your bank. Plan redemptions ahead of payroll, tax and vendor deadlines.

Is our company’s capital guaranteed?

No. These are market-linked investments, not bank deposits. Credit, interest-rate and liquidity risks remain, and values can fall. Check the scheme’s Riskometer, portfolio and documents before investing.

Is this meant to replace our long-term FDs?

Start with short-term surplus sitting in your current account. There is no need to break an existing FD. Compare each option against your cash-flow needs, risk policy and the bank’s actual quote.

Do we submit documents to every AMC?

Pazy keeps your company documents and verification steps in one digital checklist. Additional company, signatory, beneficial-owner or fund-house requirements can still apply.

How much company surplus do we need?

This offer starts from ₹5 lakh. Use cash left after operating needs, known commitments and a contingency buffer. Scheme minimums and your company’s investment policy still apply.

What do we pay, and when?

Register for free. Pay ₹199 to start onboarding. Submit documents for company KYC. Once approved, pay ₹5,000 to complete setup. Total service fees: ₹5,199. Your investment amount is separate. Taxes, refund terms and final inclusions need confirmation before live checkout.

What if KYC needs a correction?

Your checklist shows what needs fixing so you can resubmit. The ₹5,000 setup payment stays locked until approval. Paying ₹199 does not guarantee approval; refund terms are not final in this prototype.

Does Pazy hold our investment money?

Pazy facilitates transactions through BSE StAR MF. Units are held in your company’s folio with the fund house and registrar. Pazy does not pool your investment principal in its own account. Service fees are separate.
